On first launch an adult creates a 6–12 digit parent PIN and one local explorer using a nickname and Year 1–6. No online account or test credentials are required. The child home has six game modes. The review build permits the free starter selection: three bundled questions per game/year. Use choice buttons, letter tiles, sequence tiles, hints and retries. Answers show explanations.
